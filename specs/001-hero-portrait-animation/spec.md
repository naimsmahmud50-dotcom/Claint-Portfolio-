# Feature Specification: Hero Portrait Animation

## User Story
As a user, I want my portrait in the portfolio Hero section to be presented within a circle, surrounded by a rotating, animated RGB ring and revolving icons of my key skills (MS Office, Design Tools, AI Tools). This will make my portfolio more visually engaging and highlight my versatility.

## Functional Requirements
1. **Portrait Presentation**: The portrait image must be displayed within a circular container, maintaining its aspect ratio and top focus (as per existing design).
2. **Static Portrait**: The portrait image itself must remain static (not rotate).
3. **Animated RGB Ring**: A circular, glowing ring with RGB color gradients must rotate slowly around the portrait container.
4. **Revolving Skill Icons**:
   - Icons for Ps, Ai, Pr, Ae, T, AI, W, X, P must be positioned around the rotating ring.
   - These icons must revolve around the central portrait.
5. **Responsiveness & Stability**: The animation must be responsive, maintain the layout structure, and not negatively impact other components.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Portrait is contained within a circle.
- [ ] RGB ring is visible, glowing, and rotating around the portrait.
- [ ] Skill icons are positioned around the ring and are also rotating/revolving.
- [ ] The Hero section layout remains intact.
- [ ] Animation is smooth and performant.

## Out of Scope
- Changing the existing portrait image file.
- Modifying the navigation or other layout components.
